Preview your assistant

Test how your assistant answers before you put it on your website. The chat window opens in the bottom-right corner, exactly as visitors will see it.

Previewing

Try questions your customers ask, for example:

  • What services do you offer?
  • How much does it cost?
  • Something your website does not cover — the assistant should say it is not sure and offer the contact form.
  • "Please contact me at name@example.com" — the lead form should open with the mail address filled in.

Leads you submit here are real and appear in your dashboard. Messages count toward your daily limit.
