Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

MSPoint Assist is a service of MSPoint LLC, a limited liability company registered in Wyoming, USA ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information we handle when businesses use MSPoint Assist ("owners") and when people chat with an assistant on a website ("visitors").

1. Information we collect

From owners

  • Business name, website address and mail address entered when creating an assistant.
  • Settings the owner writes, such as the greeting and custom instructions.
  • The text of the owner's public web pages, which our crawler reads to answer visitor questions. We do not access password-protected pages.

From visitors

  • Chat messages are processed to generate answers. We do not keep a log of conversations, except as described in the next point.
  • Contact details a visitor chooses to submit in the lead form (name, mail address, company, phone, message), together with the page address and the last few messages of that conversation, so the business can follow up.
  • A daily count of messages per assistant, used for plan limits, and an hourly per-visitor message count used to prevent abuse. The visitor count is keyed by a one-way hash of the IP address (the IP itself is not stored) and is deleted after about two days.

Please do not share sensitive information such as passwords, payment card numbers, bank account numbers or national ID numbers in the chat.

2. How we use information

  • To answer visitor questions using the owner's website content.
  • To deliver submitted leads to the business that owns the assistant, including an automatic priority label and short summary.
  • To operate, secure and improve the service, and to prevent abuse.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use chat messages or leads to train AI models.

3. Who processes the data

MSPoint Assist runs on Cloudflare (Workers, Workers AI, Vectorize and D1), which stores and processes data on our behalf under its own data processing terms. Service mails (new-lead alerts, welcome and sign-in mails) are sent through Zoho ZeptoMail. Leads are visible only to the owner of the assistant that collected them and to MSPoint LLC for support and security purposes.

The business that installed the assistant on its website decides why it collects visitor details and how it uses them after receiving a lead. Visitors should also read that business's own privacy policy.

4. Retention and deletion

  • Website content and leads are kept until the owner deletes the assistant from the dashboard, which removes its website index, leads and usage records.
  • Visitors who want their submitted details removed can contact the business they wrote to, or mail us and we will remove them.

5. Security

Data is sent over HTTPS. Owner keys are stored only as one-way hashes. Access to production systems is limited to MSPoint LLC.

6. Children

The service is meant for businesses and is not directed at children under 16.

7. Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
